ORIGIN AND ATTRIBUTION
---------------------
This skill began from `web-design-clone`, an Anthropic-authored skill, installed
2026-07-27 from a .skill bundle and renamed `swipefile` on 2026-07-30.

That attribution is confirmed. The skill ships today as
`anthropic-skills:web-design-clone`. It is worth noting for anyone who tries to
check it that it does NOT appear in the public `anthropics/skills` repository on
GitHub - a search there comes back empty and can be mistaken for evidence that
the skill does not exist.

WHAT WAS INHERITED, MEASURED
----------------------------
Compared by 8-word shingle overlap against the current upstream text:

  of swipefile's SKILL.md, inherited        7.7%   (~68 of 888 lines)
  of the whole repository                   0.28%  (of 24,287 lines)
  of the ancestor's own body, still present 72%    (inside a document 18x its length)

Both directions matter and they say different things. The second column is why
this is fairly called a different skill; the third is why the attribution above
is not a formality. Substantial passages of the original survive largely intact.

Inherited: the Match/Adapt distinction and the argument for separating them, the
"you cannot see a live page" constraint and the capture -> spec -> build -> diff
response to it, the shape of Steps 1-4, "Copying, and where the line is", and
"Failure modes worth watching for".

Added here: three further jobs (Audit, Transfer, Brand), Step 0 (consult the
library), Step 0.5 (brief the gaps), Step 5 (record what you learned), the
fidelity bar for Match, and the portability section - plus the entire
measurement apparatus the skill is now organised around:

  library/        26 measured teardowns, several of this author's own builds
  scripts/        19 programs - crawl, build, serve, packaging, provenance,
                  the design and copy gates, motion extraction, library linting
  scripts/tests/  14 suites, 525 checks
  references/     9 procedure documents
  BUILDS.md       the log of what has actually been built with this

The first commit already carried 12 library entries and a 10-file suite, eleven
days after the install.

OTHER THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL: REFERENCED, NOT INCLUDED
----------------------------------------------------
`mengto-skills` (MIT, (c) Meng To) is pointed at by path in
`references/taste.md` as a technique library to read. None of its file content
ships here. If a build or a distributable ever includes any of it, carry the MIT
notice, per its license.

Library entries describe third-party sites by measurement - geometry, tokens,
type metrics, easing curves - and do not reproduce their assets or markup. A
Match build made with this skill is a local study and is not published; that
distinction is stated in the skill itself and is what keeps the library
publishable.
